The approach

Less memorising.
More understanding.

Chess can feel overwhelming when every position seems to demand a perfect answer. A useful learning process breaks the game into manageable questions: What is changing? What matters most? Which moves deserve a closer look?

The aim is to develop your own thinking, not just hand you an answer. Curiosity, practice, and honest review are at the heart of lasting improvement.

Lesson topics and format can be agreed together. No rating, certification, or coaching record is claimed on this page.
